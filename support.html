Support

QuietField is a quiet, private place for your thoughts. If something is not working, or you have a question, email zhoujing2026@gmail.com — we read everything.

Getting started

  • Capture a thought: type or tap the mic, then tap Capture. Voice is transcribed on-device where possible.
  • Open the Field: your thoughts appear as a connected map you can orbit, pan and pinch.
  • Layers: read the same map by Meaning, Time, People or Open Loops.
  • Apple Watch: tap the field, dictate or type, and the thought syncs to your phone.

Premium and purchases

  • Premium unlocks the full Field and related features. Both plans are auto-renewable subscriptions (yearly and monthly) with prices shown on the purchase screen.
  • To restore a purchase: Settings → Premium → Restore purchase.
  • Manage or cancel: iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Free trial: if offered, unused trial time is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

Privacy

Read our Privacy Policy. In short: no account, no tracking, thoughts stay on your device.

AI features

AI features (theme summaries, connection explanations, Ask the Field) are optional and off by default. On supported devices they use Apple Intelligence on-device. You may connect your own OpenAI-compatible provider in Settings → AI enhancements; only short excerpts of your thoughts are sent to the endpoint you choose.

Troubleshooting

  • Premium not loading? Make sure you are signed into the App Store and connected to the internet, then reopen the purchase screen. If you are on a sandbox account, switch its storefront to a country where the app is available.
  • Microphone not working? iPhone Settings → QuietField → Microphone, and allow Speech Recognition if asked.
  • Widget not updating? Open the app once to refresh the shared field snapshot, then wait for the widget to reload.
