Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

QuietField is a private journaling app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your thoughts stay on your devices. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics SDK.

1. What we collect

Nothing on our servers. We do not operate a server that stores your thoughts. QuietField does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifiers.

Your thoughts, themes, connections and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Optional iCloud sync

You may turn on iCloud sync so your thoughts appear on your other devices. When enabled, data is stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. We have no access to that data. You can turn sync off at any time; the app then works fully on-device.

3. Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only while you are actively dictating a thought. Transcription is performed on your device whenever possible. If your device cannot transcribe locally and you have allowed Apple's standard dictation, audio is processed by Apple's speech services under Apple's privacy policy. QuietField never stores audio recordings.

4. Optional AI features

Theme naming, theme summaries, connection explanations and Ask the Field can optionally use an AI language model. These features are off by default and only run when you enable them.

  • Apple Intelligence (on-device): on supported devices these features run with Apple's on-device model. Your text does not leave the device for this processing.
  • Your own AI provider (BYOK): you may connect an OpenAI-compatible provider of your choice by entering its endpoint and API key in Settings. If you enable this, short excerpts of your thoughts (typically a few thoughts at a time) are sent directly to that provider to generate the result. We do not receive or store them. The provider's own privacy policy applies. The API key is stored in your device's Keychain.

You can disable AI features at any time; the app remains fully functional without them.

5. No tracking, no ads, no analytics

QuietField contains no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not profile you, sell data or share data with ad networks.

5a. Purchases and subscriptions

QuietField offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple, not by us. We do not receive your payment details, name or billing address. App Store Connect may share anonymous, aggregated sales reports with us (for example, download and subscription counts) that cannot identify you. Subscription status is checked on your device through StoreKit so the app knows what to unlock.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device (and, if you enable it, your private iCloud). You can delete individual thoughts or all data from within the app (Settings → Delete all thoughts). Deleting the app removes local data. If you use iCloud sync, delete the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. We hold no copy to delete.

7. Children

QuietField is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, requests to access, correct or delete data held by us do not apply. You retain full control of the data on your device and in your iCloud account.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Material changes will be reflected in the app's App Store listing.
